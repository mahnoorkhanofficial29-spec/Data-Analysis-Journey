 📁 Projects

🏦 Bank Customer Churn Analysis — SQL

My first data analysis project is a SQL-based analysis of 10,000 bank customers.

The goal of this project was to understand **why customers are leaving the bank** and find patterns in the data that might be related to churn.

 🔍 What I Analyzed

* Overall customer churn rate
* Churn by geography
* Churn by gender
* Churn by age group
* Active vs. inactive customers
* Number of products
* Credit card ownership
* Credit score
* Account balance
* Customer tenure
* Estimated salary
* Gender and geography combinations

 💡 What I Found

* Germany had the highest churn rate among the three countries.
* Female customers had a higher churn rate than male customers.
* Customers aged **50–59** had the highest churn rate.
* Inactive customers were more likely to leave the bank.
* Female customers in Germany had the highest churn rate among the gender and geography groups I analyzed.
* Credit card ownership and estimated salary didn't appear to have a strong impact on churn.

Tools I used:

* MySQL
* MySQL Workbench
* SQL

📄 Project file: `bank_churn_analysis.sql`
